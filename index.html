Our values and beliefs

School is about discipline, hard work, and time management. Be sure you are seeking help and advice so you don't go astray or drift off. At Tech, we work hard to take care of the technology that enables learning and teaching.

Plug in your Chromebook after use. Do not just dump and leave. Students are in a hurry to get out, but they expect computers to be fully charged when they come back.

Chromebook repair

For Chromebook repair and most classroom support. Teachers need to complete the Tech Needs form or submit a request to the district technology services in order to get a district-assigned technician to respond to the ticket.

No tech at home?

If you need a hotspot or computer for home use, please ask your parent to complete the request in Aeries. Parents can request a student computer for home use in Aeries

How would I get my requested tech?

The tech request goes to the district technology services office where the information is processed and the requested technology is sent to the school. They will subsequently deliever it to the students' teacher where the students can pick it up during class.

My question wasn't answered, where is this information from?

The information is available through all district and school channels. It is also available in the parent portal of the district website.
